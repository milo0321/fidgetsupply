## 2. Invariants for Tiered Volume Tables
1. **Quantity Escalation & Real-World Brackets**:
   - **1,000 pcs Bags & Carrying Standard**: `1,000 - 2,999 pcs` | `3,000 - 4,999 pcs` | `5,000 - 9,999 pcs` | `10,000+ pcs (Enterprise)`
   - **500 pcs Standard**: `500 - 999 pcs` | `1,000 - 2,999 pcs` | `3,000 - 4,999 pcs` | `5,000+ pcs (Enterprise)`
   - **300 pcs Technical/Apparel**: `300 - 499 pcs` | `500 - 999 pcs` | `1,000 - 2,999 pcs` | `3,000+ pcs (Enterprise)`
   - **100 sets VIP Sets**: `100 - 299 sets` | `300 - 999 sets` | `1,000 - 2,999 sets` | `3,000+ sets (Enterprise)`
2. **Margin Scaling & Volume Gradient Decay Bounds (20% to 35% Spread Invariant)**:
   - FOB Unit Prices must decrease realistically with volume.
   - **Strict Spread Invariant**: The total discount from Tier 1 (MOQ) to Tier 4 (Enterprise) MUST strictly stay within **20% to 35%** (at most 38% for small high-setup hardware like pins).
   - **Mathematical Model**: Tier 1: $1.00 \times P_{\text{base}}$, Tier 2: $0.82–0.85 \times P_{\text{base}}$, Tier 3: $0.72–0.75 \times P_{\text{base}}$, Tier 4: $0.65–0.70 \times P_{\text{base}}$.
   - **Prohibited Anti-Pattern**: Extreme tier drops (>40% plunge) and flat pricing (<15% drop) are strictly forbidden, as they destroy buyer trust and resemble scam/bait-and-switch pricing.
3. **Turnaround Realism**: Sample times are 3-7 business days; bulk production times are 10-25 days depending on labor complexity. Never promise impossible "1-day production" for custom cut-and-sew apparel or molded hardware.
4. **Anti-Scam Differentiated Sample Fee Refund Policy (差异化样品费返还门槛)**:
   - **No Unconditional Free Proof**: Never offer upfront free samples without payment to prevent freebie fraud.
   - **100% Invoice Credit**: Sample development fees are 100% credited back against the final bulk invoice upon order confirmation.
   - **Mandatory Table Subtitle Statement**:
     `Tiered factory-direct volume pricing and production turnaround times. Sample development fees are 100% credited back against your final invoice upon bulk order confirmation.`
   - **Differentiated Category Thresholds**:
     - *High-Tooling / High-Setup* (Pins, Smart Drinkware, Tech Backpacks, Softshells, Bio-Leathers, Executive Sets): `Fee Refundable` applies ONLY at $\ge 3,000$ pcs (Tier 4).
     - *Mass Low-Value* (Lanyards, Wristbands, Gaiters, Basic Pouches, Dog Tags, Lint Rollers): `Fee Refundable` applies ONLY at $\ge 5,000$ pcs (Tier 4).
     - *Standard Bulk* (Canvas Totes, Stainless Tumblers, Standard Apparel, Pet Gear): `Fee Refundable` applies at $\ge 1,000$ pcs (Tier 3 & Tier 4).

## 3. Strict Invariants: No B2C Micro-Order Pricing & Realistic Unit Economics

1. **Prohibition of B2C Micro-Orders**: Never specify unrealistic micro-order pricing (such as `MOQ 100 at $0.28 = $28 total order value`). Custom manufacturing requires plate making, silkscreen mesh setup, dyeing minimums, export carton packaging, and international banking fees that make micro-orders financially non-viable.
2. **Category Minimum Order Value (MOV) Thresholds**:
   - **Mass Accessories (Wristbands, Lanyards, Pins, Keychains, Pouches, Lint Rollers)**:
     - Factory MOQ: **300 - 500 pcs** (1,000 pcs for Tyvek paper bands).
     - Realistic FOB Unit Price: **$0.35 - $1.85 / pc**.
     - Minimum Order Value (MOV): **$\ge$ $250 - $500**.
   - **Bags, Drinkware & Pet Sourcing (Canvas Totes, Jute Bags, Tumblers, Dog Harnesses)**:
     - Factory MOQ: **300 - 500 pcs**.
     - Realistic FOB Unit Price: **$1.20 - $8.50 / pc**.
     - Minimum Order Value (MOV): **$\ge$ $500 - $1,500**.
   - **Apparel, Outerwear, Bio-Leathers & VIP Gift Sets**:
     - Factory MOQ: **100 - 300 pcs / sets**.
     - Realistic FOB Unit Price: **$5.50 - $25.00 / pc**.
     - Minimum Order Value (MOV): **$\ge$ $1,500 - $3,000**.

## 4. Strict Unit Economics & Gross Profit Margin Consistency (出厂单价与制造成本严密校验)

1. **Mathematical Profit Margin Invariant (35% - 50% Gross Margin Target)**:
   - Every FOB wholesale price in `.procurement-table` MUST be strictly higher than the direct factory manufacturing cost (Raw Fabric/Molds + Dyeing/Printing + Assembly Labor + QC Inspection + Export Packaging).
   - **Mandatory 35% to 50% Margin Buffer**: Pricing models MUST guarantee a **35% to 50% factory gross profit margin** across all volume tiers to fully absorb environmental compliance costs (EU PPWR, GOTS/GRS chain-of-custody audits, REACH testing), factory overhead, energy, scrap rate buffers, and currency/tariff fluctuations:
     $$\text{FOB Price} = \frac{\text{Direct Manufacturing Cost}}{1 - \text{Gross Margin (0.35 - 0.50)}}$$
   - **Prohibited Anti-Pattern**: Never quote FOB prices below direct manufacturing costs (e.g., direct cost $1.56 but quoted $0.95 is an invalid negative margin and strictly forbidden).

2. **Strictly Decreasing Volume Pricing & Conflict Elimination**:
   - Unit prices MUST strictly decrease as order volume increases ($P_{\text{tier 1}} > P_{\text{tier 2}} > P_{\text{tier 3}} > P_{\text{tier 4}}$).
   - Never allow duplicate conflicting pricing tables on the same page.

3. **Structured Data & Schema Sync**:
   - When generating `Product` or `AggregateOffer` Schema.org JSON-LD, `lowPrice` and `highPrice` MUST strictly correspond to the 5,000+ Enterprise Tier price and the MOQ tier price respectively, ensuring authentic B2B price discovery in Google SERPs.

## 5. Generic SKU Differentiation Invariant (泛品类单品分型与真实交期/起订量原则)

1. **Dual-Variant Demarcation in Procurement Tables**:
   - Every `.procurement-table` MUST provide clear column headers separating:
     - **Variant A (Standard / Promotional Spec)**: e.g., Single-compartment 210D/600D polyester, standard silkscreen print, off-the-shelf plastic clips.
     - **Variant B (Technical / Premium / Pro Spec)**: e.g., Multi-panel 900D Cordura, waterproof YKK taped zippers, internal organizer compartments, laser MOLLE webbing, magnetic tactical buckles.
2. **Factory Economics & Lead Time Realism**:

   - **Technical Outerwear & 3-Layer Bonded Jackets**: MOQ **300 pcs**, Sample **7 - 10 days**, Mass Production **22 - 28 days**.
   - **Plush Mascots with Articulated Skeletons / Outfits**: MOQ **300 pcs**, Sample **5 - 7 days**, Mass Production **18 - 25 days** (Mascot Costume Heads: **20 pcs**, **25 - 30 days**).
   - **VIP Executive Presentation Sets**: MOQ **100 sets**, Sample **5 - 7 days**, Mass Production **16 - 20 days**.
3. **100% Frontmatter to Table Synchronization**:
   - The frontmatter `moq` MUST match the exact starting quantity of Tier 1 in the table.
   - The frontmatter `sampleTime` and `leadTime` MUST reflect the authentic production cycle of the factory archetype. Zero fabricated micro-lead times or flat uncalibrated numbers.
