# Factory Workshop & QC Photography Authenticity Invariants

To ensure all visual assets reflect genuine Chinese direct-factory manufacturing capabilities, build authentic enterprise buyer trust, and avoid artificial or stock-photo cliches:

## 1. Chinese Personnel or Zero-Human Product Photography
- **Default to Zero-Human Product & Craftsmanship Shots**: Prefer pure product flatlays, application mockups on real garments/gear, macro edge/stitch details, and machinery close-ups WITHOUT people. This eliminates AI human generation artifacts and preserves 100% focus on merchandise quality.
- **Strict Invariant When People Are Depicted**: Any illustration or photography featuring factory workers, loom operators, pattern engineers, quality control (QC) inspectors, or testing technicians MUST strictly depict authentic Chinese personnel. NEVER depict Caucasian or non-Chinese personnel in Chinese factory settings.
- **Zero-Tolerance Fallback**: If an image prompt or model output depicts non-Chinese workers in a factory environment, it MUST be discarded immediately and replaced with a pure product or craftsmanship visual asset.
- **Factory Apparel**: When Chinese personnel are depicted, they must wear realistic workshop uniforms (e.g., navy/blue workshop shirts, work aprons, clean-room caps, or white cotton inspection gloves).

## 2. Real Factory Workshop Environments over Sterile Laboratories
- **Minimize/Eliminate Sterile "White-Coat Laboratory" Imagery**: Promotional merchandise, apparel, drinkware, bags, and plush toys are physical manufactured products. NEVER generate generic, sterile, clinical laboratories with test tubes, beakers, or sci-fi scientist aesthetics.
- **Real Manufacturing & Quality Environments**: Depict genuine OEM/ODM industrial settings:
  - Computerized multi-head embroidery workshops (Tajima / Barudan).
  - Industrial fabric laser-cutting and pattern-drafting tables.
  - Inline sewing and assembly conveyor lines.
  - Quality assurance workbenches equipped with stainless steel digital calipers, precision unit scales, 15 lbs mechanical tension pull test clamps, and conveyor electromagnetic needle detectors.

## 3. High-Fidelity Machinery & Material Authenticity
- Focus on close-up tactile material craftsmanship, stitching density, Pantone thread matching, metal hardware electroplating, and authentic factory packaging lines (FSC cartons, vacuum sealers, barcode hangtag attachment).
