## 1. Why Silk-Screen & UV Surface Printing Are NOT Recommended on Metal Fidgets
- **Physical Root Cause**: Polished, electroplated, anodized (6061/6063 aluminum), and PVD-coated metal surfaces are ultra-smooth and chemically inert (`low surface energy / poor mechanical ink adhesion`).
- **Failure Mode**: Surface-applied silk-screen or flatbed UV ink cannot mechanically bite into smooth metal and easily peels, flakes, or scratches off under continuous everyday thumb friction and pocket carry.
- **Copywriting Rule**:
  - **NEVER** recommend silk-screen printing or surface UV printing as a positive customization option on metal fidget hardware.
  - Whenever silk-screen or UV printing is mentioned in educational comparisons or FAQs, explicitly state that **our factory does NOT recommend surface silk-screen or UV ink printing on smooth metal fidgets due to poor ink adhesion and peeling under thumb friction**.
