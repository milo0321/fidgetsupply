# B2B & Developer Jargon Elimination Policy

Never use repetitive or robotic "B2B" terms (e.g., "B2B Sourcing", "B2B Website", "B2B Managers", "B2B Playbook") or internal database/developer terms (e.g., "SKU", "Leaf Product", "Tank Page", "L3/L4 Subcategory") in user-facing headlines, CTA buttons, badges, or section subtitles. 

Authentic enterprise wholesale and manufacturing platforms do not label themselves with generic "B2B" tags or internal inventory codes.

## Invariants:
1. **Replace "B2B" with Professional Enterprise Procurement Phrasing**:
   - Use `Corporate Procurement`
   - Use `Enterprise Sourcing Infrastructure`
   - Use `Direct Factory Supply Chain`
   - Use `Senior Corporate Sourcing Specialists`
   - Use `Volume Order Quotes`
   - Use `Manufacturing Intelligence & Playbooks`
2. **Prohibit Internal "SKU" & Developer Jargon in User-Facing Copy**:
   - ❌ Never write `View [Product] SKU →` or `Explore Specific SKU Collections`.
   - ✅ Always use buyer-centric action verbs:
     - `Explore [Product Name] →` (e.g. `Explore Custom Dog Car Seat Covers →`)
     - `Explore Specific Product Collections`
     - `View Product Specifications →`
     - `Request Wholesale Pricing & Proofs →`
3. **Context Exceptions**:
   - "B2B" and "SKU" are permitted ONLY inside internal code comments, variable names, JSON schema metadata, or technical specifications when discussing ERP/inventory mapping.
