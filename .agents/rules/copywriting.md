# B2B Copywriting and Positioning Rules

When writing or modifying any content (copywriting, design, strategic positioning) for the Ruixing Gifts B2B website, you **MUST STRICTLY** adhere to the following rules. The user has defined this as the absolute bottom line for the website's credibility.

## 1. NO Exaggeration or Unrealistic Hype
- **Do not** use overblown, "high-brow" (高逼格), or Silicon Valley-style startup jargon (e.g., "Next-Generation", "Innovation Lab", "Revolutionary", "World-Changing").
- **Do not** claim to possess massive facilities or capabilities that do not match the reality of a Sourcing Partner/Trading Company. (e.g., Do not say "Material Innovation Lab" when the reality is just "Sustainable Material Sourcing").
- If it sounds like a pitch for a billion-dollar tech company rather than a reliable Chinese manufacturing partner, it is **wrong**.

## 2. Authentic & Grounded Sourcing Partner Identity (FIRST IRON RULE)
- Speak as a highly professional, reliable, and grounded **Global Sourcing Partner / Supply Chain Ecosystem** in China.
- **NEVER** write "Direct Factory Manufacturing", "Direct Manufacturer", or claim that Ruixing Gifts is a single direct mega-factory. We are an expert B2B supply chain consolidation partner.
- Your true strengths are your 50+ audited factory network, independent QC process (AQL 2.5), certified material expertise (GOTS, GRS, BSCI), cross-category consolidation, and turnkey DDP shipping. Present these truthfully and professionally without inflating or misrepresenting our identity.

## 3. Pragmatic Tone
- The tone must be pragmatic, trustworthy, and solution-oriented. 
- B2B buyers value honesty and capability over flashy but empty promises. Unrealistic rhetoric destroys trust; grounded facts build it.

## 4. The Bottom Line (Reality Check)
- **Authenticity is the absolute bottom line.** Any content generated must pass the "reality check" of a standard, high-quality Chinese B2B trading company. 
- Over-promising or using exaggerated rhetoric will severely backfire and destroy the trust of procurement teams. Always lean towards being humble, precise, and highly capable.

## 5. De-AI Copywriting & Robotic Buzzword Prohibition
- **Strictly Prohibited Buzzwords**: Never use formulaic AI clichés across insight articles, landing pages, or product copy, including:
  - `delve`, `dive into`, `deep dive`
  - `more than just a...`, `not just a...`
  - `elevate your brand`, `unlock the potential`
  - `paramount`, `meticulous`, `seamlessly`, `plethora`
  - `in today's fast-paced world / landscape`
- **Factual, Active Voice**: Replace vague marketing buzzwords with concrete manufacturing parameters (fabric GSM, seam tensile ratings, PMS color matching tolerances, AQL 2.5 acceptance thresholds).

## 6. Strict 100% Full English Invariant (Zero Non-English Text)
- All generated articles (`src/content/insights/*.md`), product specs and descriptions (`src/content/products/*.json`), page components (`src/**/*.astro`), HTML tags, and metadata MUST be written **100% in pure, grammatically flawless English**.
- **Zero Chinese Characters**: Never allow Chinese characters (`[\u4e00-\u9fa5]`) anywhere in user-facing content, schema markup, or internal source templates.
- **Rule 17 Compliance**: The codebase is continuously guarded by Rule 17 in `scripts/verify_all_rules.cjs`.
