<h2 id="magnetic-array-vs-ceramic-detent-bead-coins">3. Internal Kinematics: Pure 12-Magnet N52/N54 Coins vs. Hybrid Ceramic-Bead &amp; R188 Bearing Coins</h2>

As analyzed in our <a href="/insights/n35-vs-n52-neodymium-magnet-haptic-feel-guide">N35 vs. N52 Neodymium Magnet Guide</a> and <a href="/insights/ptfe-teflon-tape-ceramic-beads-r188-bearing-fidget-maintenance-guide">PTFE &amp; Ceramic Bead Tribology Guide</a>, metal haptic coins fall into three distinct mechanical sub-architectures:

<ol>
  <li><strong>Pure Radial Magnetic Clicker Coins (<a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013</a>, <a href="/products/sandwich-cookie-textured-haptic-coin">FS-027</a>, <a href="/products/poker-suit-engraved-haptic-coin">FS-033</a>, <a href="/products/mechanical-pumpkin-n54-haptic-coin">FS-053</a>):</strong> Rely on 12 opposing neodymium magnets arranged around a low-friction axial thrust washer. Because the two metal plates never grind against mechanical teeth, the magnetic snap remains zero-wear across 500,000+ rotations and produces a deep, springy resistance curve.</li>
  <li><strong>Hybrid N54 Magnet + Spring-Loaded Ceramic Bead Ratchet Coins (<a href="/products/tiger-crest-ceramic-bead-haptic-coin">FS-041</a>):</strong> Combines high-flux N54 neodymium magnets with silicon-nitride ($Si_3N_4$) ceramic detent balls riding over precision-milled dimples. This creates a sharp, mechanical "ratchet wrench" acoustic click beloved by TikTok ASMR creators.</li>
  <li><strong>2-in-1 Haptic Clicker + R188 Free-Spinning Gyro Coins (<a href="/products/wheel-rim-12-magnet-haptic-coin">FS-030</a>, <a href="/products/invader-heavyweight-r188-haptic-coin">FS-071</a>, <a href="/products/6063-aluminum-coin-top-desktop-gyro">FS-139</a>):</strong> Integrates a 12-magnet ratcheting outer ring with an independent R188 ball-bearing center hub—giving the user both a tactile thumb clicker and a high-speed desktop spinner in one coin.</li>
</ol>
