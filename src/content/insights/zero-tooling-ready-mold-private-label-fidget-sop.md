<h2 id="how-we-sanitized-140-skus-for-ip-safety">3. How We Audited &amp; Deduplicated 148 Factory Rows into 140 IP-Safe Models</h2>

Raw Chinese factory spreadsheets are notorious for duplicate rows, out-of-stock items, and risky pop-culture nicknames that trigger intellectual-property (IP) bots on Amazon and Shopify.

Before publishing our master catalog (`FS-001` to `FS-150`), our engineering team executed a complete **Catalog Hygiene &amp; IP Sanitization Protocol**:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Eliminated True Duplicates &amp; Out-of-Stock Rows:</strong> We removed duplicate row <code>#147</code> (100% identical to <a href="/products/aluminum-brass-16-magnet-spinner-ring">FS-135 Spinner Ring</a>) and archived out-of-stock row <code>#65</code> (Iron Lucky Bead Roller) so every listed SKU is actively producible.</li>
  <li><strong>Consolidated Multi-Material Same-Mold Families:</strong> Rather than cluttering the catalog with separate pages for the same mold in different metals, we merged 6 multi-material families—such as the <a href="/products/lake-light-sword-multi-metal-mechanical-edc">FS-114 / FS-115 / FS-132 Lake-Light Sword</a> (Zinc Alloy vs. 304 Stainless vs. Zirconium) and <a href="/products/celestial-relief-multi-metal-gourd-edc">FS-105 / FS-106 Celestial Gourd</a>—into unified product pages with interactive material tier selectors.</li>
  <li><strong>Separated Same-Name Different-Mold Sliders:</strong> We distinguished the two different Piano Sliders by their true engineering specs: <a href="/products/compact-16-magnet-piano-haptic-slider">FS-018 (Compact 16-Magnet Dual-Layer Piano Slider, 60×38×9mm)</a> versus <a href="/products/elongated-32-magnet-piano-track-slider">FS-048 (Elongated 32-Magnet Multi-Key Piano Track Slider, 73.5×23×11.5mm)</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>100% IP-Safe Naming &amp; Clean Unmarked Hardware:</strong> All third-party brand nicknames (such as <em>Oreo</em>, <em>Pac-Man</em>, <em>Thor</em>, <em>Elden Ring</em>, or <em>01EDC</em>) were replaced with clean geometric descriptors (e.g., <a href="/products/sandwich-cookie-textured-haptic-coin">FS-027 Sandwich Cookie Textured Coin</a>), and all units ship free of unauthorized third-party studio markings.</li>
</ul>
