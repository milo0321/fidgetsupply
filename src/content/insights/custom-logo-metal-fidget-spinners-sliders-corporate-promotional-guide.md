<h2 id="why-we-recommend-fiber-laser-and-deep-color-fill-over-ink">3. Engineering Truth: Why Custom Fidget Spinners With Company Logo Must Use Laser or Recessed Enamel Instead of Surface Ink</h2>

When buyers request a multi-color **fidget spinner logo** or branded magnetic slider, general promotional trading companies frequently quote cheap surface UV flatbed printing or pad printing. Within 10 days of actual use, that surface ink flakes off.

Here is the metallurgical reason why surface ink fails on metal fidgets—and how our factory solves it permanently:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Why Surface Silk-Screen &amp; UV Ink Peel Off Metal:</strong> Electroplated chrome/gunmetal Zamak #3 (<a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125</a>, <a href="/products/chocolate-grid-20-magnet-n52-unlimited-slider">FS-128</a>), Type-III hard-anodized 6063 aluminum (<a href="/products/minimalist-smooth-6063-aluminum-slider">FS-047</a>, <a href="/products/anodized-aluminum-12-magnet-gourd-edc">FS-096</a>), and CNC-polished 304 stainless steel (<a href="/products/gen2-304ss-streamlined-goose-spinner">FS-134</a>, <a href="/products/asymmetric-swept-wing-stainless-spinner">FS-137</a>) have ultra-low surface porosity and low surface energy. Because a fidget spinner or magnetic slider experiences 500 to 2,000 thumb strokes per day combined with natural skin oils, surface-sitting ink delaminates along the edges.</li>
  <li><strong>Solution A — 50W MOPA Fiber Laser Engraving (+$0.15–$0.30/pc, MOQ 100 pcs, $50 Sample Fee):</strong> Our 50W fiber laser vaporizes the anodized oxide layer or micro-etches the stainless steel substrate to a depth of 0.03–0.06mm. On matte black or navy anodized aluminum (<a href="/products/minimalist-smooth-6063-aluminum-slider">FS-047</a>, <a href="/products/6-ball-orbit-rainbow-aluminum-spinner">FS-074</a>, <a href="/products/m-profile-aluminum-16-magnet-ring">FS-140</a>), the laser reveals a crisp, permanent silver-white metallic logo with 0.02mm edge precision.</li>
  <li><strong>Solution B — Deep Laser Engraving + Pantone Enamel Color Fill (+$0.35–$0.60/pc, MOQ 100–200 pcs, $80–$150 Sample Fee):</strong> When your corporate brand guidelines require exact Pantone (PMS) colors on metal, we use multiple high-power fiber laser passes to carve a <strong>0.18mm to 0.25mm recessed trench</strong> into the metal housing (<a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125</a>, <a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013</a>, <a href="/products/interlocking-planetary-gear-spinner">FS-070</a>). Technicians then syringe-fill the recessed cavity with two-part epoxy enamel and bake-cure it. Because the colored pigment sits <em>below</em> the metal surface plane, your thumb slides across the surrounding metal rim and never abrades the colored logo.</li>
</ul>
