<h2 id="why-surface-metallurgy-determines-long-term-edc-value">1. Why Surface Metallurgy Determines Whether an EDC Fidget Ages Like a Luxury Watch or Peels in 30 Days</h2>

An everyday carry (EDC) fidget slider, coin, or spinner endures one of the harshest tribological environments of any consumer product: **2,000+ thumb cycles per day**, constant exposure to **human palmar sweat (`pH 4.5–6.8` with sodium chloride and lactic acid)**, and direct pocket abrasion against steel house keys and titanium pocket-knife clips.

If a supplier sprays cheap wet lacquer or single-layer flash plating onto a metal shell, the finish will blister, flake, or rub down to bare gray metal within three weeks.

Across our 140 factory molds (detailed in our <a href="/insights/zinc-alloy-die-casting-vs-cnc-stainless-steel-edc-cost">Zinc Alloy vs. CNC Stainless Steel Cost Guide</a>), we match every base alloy—**Zamak #3 Zinc Alloy**, **6063-T6 Aerospace Aluminum**, **304/316 Stainless Steel**, and **Zr702 Zirconium**—with industrial surface treatments engineered to pass **48-to-96 hour ASTM B117 neutral salt-spray and artificial perspiration testing**.

<h2 id="relieved-antique-electroplating-for-3d-sculpted-relief-molds">3. How 4-Stage Relieved Antique Electroplating Brings 3D Sculpted Molds to Life</h2>

Over 70 of our die-cast Zamak #3 sliders and coins in our <a href="/collections/magnetic-fidget-sliders">Magnetic Fidget Sliders</a> and <a href="/collections/haptic-fidget-coins">Haptic Fidget Coins</a> collections feature intricate 3D relief artwork—such as <a href="/products/poker-king-magnetic-slider">FS-001 (Poker King)</a>, <a href="/products/gothic-skull-relief-push-slider">FS-010 (Gothic Skull)</a>, <a href="/products/ancient-bronze-mask-haptic-coin">FS-017 (Ancient Bronze Mask Coin)</a>, and <a href="/products/royal-king-queen-poker-magnetic-slider">FS-024 (Royal King/Queen)</a>.

If you plate a 3D sculpture in uniform bright chrome, glare washes out all the fine engraving detail. Instead, our electroplating line executes a **4-stage museum-grade relieved antique process**:
1. **Alkaline Copper + Semi-Bright Nickel Base (`12 microns`)**: Seals the die-cast zinc substrate and creates a metallurgical diffusion barrier.
2. **Electrochemical Bronze / Silver / Gunmetal Deposit**: Plates the true metallic color layer across all peaks and valleys.
3. **Chemical Patination &amp; Hand Cotton-Wheel Relieving**: The shell is immersed in an oxidizing bath to turn the entire surface dark espresso/charcoal, then hand-buffed on a soft sisal/cotton wheel. The wheel buffs away the dark oxide **only on the raised 3D highlights**, leaving deep dark shadows inside the recessed grooves for dramatic chiaroscuro depth!
4. **Cathodic Electrophoretic Clear Coat (`E-Coat` Baked at `160°C`)**: Seals the relieved patina beneath an invisible, sweat-proof polyurethane-acrylic film that prevents green copper verdigris or finger tarnish.

<h2 id="ceramic-stonewashing-vs-pvd-on-cnc-stainless-steel">4. Ceramic Stonewashing vs. Vacuum PVD on 304/316 CNC Stainless Steel</h2>

For premium models in our <a href="/collections/cnc-stainless-steel-pei-edc">CNC Stainless Steel &amp; Exotic Metals collection</a> (<a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013</a>, <a href="/products/316ss-bionics-goose-fidget-spinner">FS-063</a>, <a href="/products/304ss-akq-triple-layer-24-magnet-slider">FS-075</a>, <a href="/products/harmonic-rhythm-cnc-stainless-slider">FS-086</a>, <a href="/products/rainbow-pvd-stainless-gimbal-gyro-model-9">FS-120</a>), buyers typically choose between two zero-peel finishes:

- **Isotropic Ceramic Media Stonewashing (Tumbled Matte)**: After 5-axis CNC milling, stainless steel parts are tumbled for 4 hours inside rotary barrels filled with `4mm` triangular porcelain-ceramic media and mild surfactant. The ceramic pyramids micro-peen every corner (`R0.3mm` chamfer), erasing CNC toolpath marks and creating an organic, non-directional matte patina that **never shows fingerprints or pocket scratches**.
- **Vacuum Magnetron Sputtering PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition)**: Inside a high-vacuum chamber (`10⁻⁴ Pa` at `350°C`), vaporized titanium, zirconium, or carbon ions bond atomically to the stainless steel surface—producing **Matte Black DLC**, **Gold TiN**, or **Iridescent Oil-Slick Rainbow** (<a href="/products/rainbow-pvd-stainless-gimbal-gyro-model-9">FS-120</a>) with a surface hardness of `2,000+ HV` (harder than quartz sand).

### Quality Control Testing Protocol: Cross-Hatch Adhesion (`ASTM D3359`) &amp; Artificial Sweat Immersion (`ISO 3160-2`)
Before any electroplated, anodized, or PVD-coated production batch is cleared for final magnet assembly at our Dongguan plant, our surface-metallurgy QC laboratory pulls 5 sample shells per 500-piece lot for destructive and non-destructive coating verification. Using an X-ray fluorescence (`XRF`) coating thickness analyzer, technicians verify that copper-nickel base layers exceed `12 microns` and anodic oxide layers exceed `18 microns`. Next, samples undergo an **ASTM D3359 cross-hatch tape adhesion test** (scoring a `1mm × 1mm` lattice into the finish and pulling with high-tack pressure-sensitive tape to verify `5B` zero-flaking adhesion) followed by a 24-hour **ISO 3160-2 artificial human sweat immersion bath** at `40°C`—guaranteeing that your customers never experience green finger staining, clear-coat delamination, or premature finish fade.
