By contrast, when our engineering team manufactures the <a href="/products/tiger-crest-spring-tactical-sword-edc">FS-104 Tiger-Crest Spring Tactical Sword EDC</a>, the <a href="/products/lake-light-sword-multi-metal-mechanical-edc">FS-114 Lake-Light Sword Multi-Metal Mechanical EDC</a>, and the <a href="/products/templar-shield-12-magnet-haptic-slider">FS-052 Templar Shield 12-Magnet Haptic Slider</a>, our factory floor enforces four mandatory production invariants across every batch:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Sub-0.02 mm CNC &amp; Die-Cast Datum Tolerances:</strong> All bearing seats, hinge pivot bores, track guide channels, and magnet cavities are precision-reamed or CNC-turned to <code>&plusmn;0.015 mm</code> concentricity. This prevents axial bind, lateral plate rock, or premature gear tooth wear on complex assemblies like the <a href="/products/gothic-horned-shield-haptic-coin">FS-034 Gothic Horned Shield Haptic Coin</a> and <a href="/products/corsair-hammer-triple-layer-ptfe-slider">FS-123 Corsair Hammer Triple-Layer PTFE Slider</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Captive N52 Neodymium &amp; Hardened Spring Retention:</strong> Every neodymium magnetic array (graded from N45 to N54) and 301 stainless steel leaf or coil spring is mechanically staked, recessed under CNC retaining plates, or sealed inside dual-wall structural chambers.   <li><strong>Surface Hardness &amp; Anti-Fingerprint PVD/Anodized Coatings:</strong> All external contact surfaces undergo multi-stage ceramic vibratory tumbling, fine glass-bead blasting, vacuum PVD titanium nitride plating, or Type-III hard anodizing as detailed in our <a href="/insights/pvd-coating-hard-anodizing-stonewash-antique-metal-fidget-finishes">PVD Coating, Hard Anodizing &amp; Stonewash Finishes Guide</a>.
