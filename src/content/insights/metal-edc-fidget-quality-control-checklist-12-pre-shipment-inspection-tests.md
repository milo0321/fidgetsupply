For Amazon FBA sellers, specialty EDC retailers, and corporate gift buyers, catching these mechanical anomalies <em>before</em> cartons leave China is the difference between a profitable reorder cycle and a wave of customer returns.</p>

Every production batch across our <a href="/collections/magnetic-fidget-sliders">Magnetic Haptic Sliders</a>, <a href="/collections/tuning-fork-fidget-sliders">Acoustic Tuning Fork Sliders</a>, and <a href="/collections/metal-fidget-spinners-gyros">Metal Fidget Spinners &amp; Desktop Gyros</a> passes through a standardized <strong>12-Point Pre-Shipment Inspection Checklist</strong> at our Fuzhou Export Logistics Hub before packing photos and QC videos are sent to the buyer for final dispatch sign-off.</p>

<h2>1. Domain A: Magnetic Flux Density, Polarity Array &amp; Captive Retention Tests (Tests #1 to #3)</h2>

<p>Magnetic sliders, clicker coins, and spinner rings depend on precise magnetic attraction and repulsion curves. When assembling high-density models such as the <a href="/products/heart-matrix-26-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-056 Heart-Matrix 26-Magnet N50 PTFE Unlimited Slider</a> ($4.56–$5.62), <a href="/products/abalone-inlay-56-magnet-stainless-slider">FS-091 Abalone Shell Inlay 56-Magnet N52 Stainless Acoustic Slider</a> ($22.96–$28.34), or <a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125 Smooth Soap-Box 20-Magnet N52 Unlimited Haptic Slider</a> ($3.53–$4.36), our metrology technicians execute three mandatory magnetic inspections:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Test #1 — Digital Tesla Gaussmeter Surface Flux Verification:</strong> Using a calibrated Hall-effect probe before and after magnet seating, technicians verify that raw neodymium cylinders meet the exact grade specified on the SKU Bill of Materials—distinguishing standard N35/N40 track magnets (2,800–3,200 Gauss surface field) from high-energy N50/N52/N54 unlimited slider magnets (3,800–4,300+ Gauss). Compare grade behaviors in our <a href="/insights/n35-vs-n52-neodymium-magnet-haptic-feel-guide">N35 vs. N52 Neodymium Magnet Haptic Feel Guide</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>Test #2 — Optical Polarity Viewing Film &amp; Master Jig Alignment:</strong> In multi-row sliders like the <a href="/products/triple-layer-32-magnet-slider">FS-002 Triple-Layer 32-Magnet Tactile Haptic Slider</a> ($2.33–$2.88) and <a href="/products/elongated-32-magnet-piano-track-slider">FS-048 Elongated 32-Magnet Piano Track Haptic Slider</a> ($4.14–$5.12), a single flipped North/South pole creates a dead spot where the slider repels instead of snapping into detent. Every assembled plate is scanned beneath a green magnetic field viewing film to confirm 100% uniform pole symmetry.</li>
  <li><strong>Test #3 — 1.5-Meter Concrete Drop &amp; Thermal Cycle Magnet Retention Test:</strong> To guarantee compliance with <a href="/insights/us-cpsc-16-cfr-1262-magnet-fidget-safety-compliance">US CPSC 16 CFR 1262 magnet safety regulations</a>, sample units from each curing batch undergo 10 repeated drops from 1.5 meters onto a steel-backed impact plate after 24 hours of epoxy curing or Loctite 243 screw torqueing. Zero magnet loosening or cover-plate shift is permitted.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. Domain B: Mechanical Kinematics, R188 Spin Duration &amp; Acoustic Resonance Tests (Tests #4 to #8)</h2>

<p>Moving parts—whether linear PTFE tracks, spring-loaded ratchets, or high-RPM rotational bearings—must feel crisp and consistent across every single unit in a 500-piece carton:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Test #4 — Track Flatness &amp; PTFE Teflon Pad Seating:</strong> On unlimited sliders such as the <a href="/products/carbon-fiber-brass-16-magnet-slider">FS-038 Carbon Fiber &amp; Brass Inlay 16-Magnet Featherweight Slider</a> ($22.58–$27.86) and <a href="/products/conqueror-ultra-slim-26-magnet-ss-slider">FS-144 Conqueror Ultra-Slim 5.2mm 26-Magnet CNC Stainless Slider</a> ($10.36–$12.78), plates are lapped on optical granite surface plates so self-lubricating PTFE stickers sit 100% flush without edge peeling. Read our <a href="/insights/ptfe-teflon-tape-ceramic-beads-r188-bearing-fidget-maintenance-guide">PTFE Tape, Ceramic Beads &amp; R188 Bearing Maintenance Guide</a> for tolerance specs.</li>
  <li><strong>Test #5 — Acoustic Tuning Fork sustain vs. Quiet Office Decibel Check:</strong> Acoustic resonance models—including the <a href="/products/harmonic-rhythm-cnc-stainless-slider">FS-086 Harmonic Rhythm CNC Stainless Steel Acoustic Slider</a> ($20.89–$25.78), <a href="/products/acoustic-chime-multi-metal-gourd-edc">FS-107 / FS-112 Acoustic Resonance Chime Dual-Bearing Multi-Metal Gourd</a> ($23.38–$144.33), <a href="/products/resonance-cnc-stainless-tuning-fork-spinner">FS-148 Resonance Chamber CNC Stainless Acoustic Tuning Fork Spinner</a> ($19.04–$23.50), and <a href="/products/compact-20-magnet-acoustic-tuning-fork-slider">FS-150 Compact 20-Magnet N45 PTFE Acoustic Tuning Fork Slider</a> ($6.00–$7.41)—are strike-tested to ensure the cantilever tines ring freely without screw-thread damping.</li>
  <li><strong>Test #6 — Multi-Action Spring &amp; Ratchet Detent Indexing:</strong> Complex mechanical assemblies like the <a href="/products/bolt-action-spring-breech-edc-slider">FS-067 Bolt-Action Rifle Breech Spring-Loaded Stainless &amp; Zinc EDC</a> ($7.87–$9.71), <a href="/products/bamboo-joint-aluminum-ratchet-edc">FS-092 Bamboo-Joint CNC Aluminum Dual-Spring Ratchet EDC</a> ($8.70–$10.73), and <a href="/products/drop-tower-6-bearing-24-magnet-heavy-edc">FS-142 Drop-Tower 6-Bearing 24-Magnet Brass &amp; Zinc Heavyweight EDC</a> ($20.71–$25.55) are hand-cycled through all directional stops to verify crisp detent return.</li>
  <li><strong>Test #7 — R188 Bearing Free-Spin Duration (>= 180s) &amp; Axial Runout:</strong> Every rotational model—such as the <a href="/products/316ss-bionics-goose-fidget-spinner">FS-063 316 Stainless Steel Dual-Bearing Bionics Goose Spinner</a> ($37.16–$45.85), <a href="/products/6-axis-cnc-stainless-gyro-spinner">FS-066 6-Axis CNC Stainless Steel Mechanical Gyro Spinner</a> ($31.06–$38.33), and <a href="/products/6063-aluminum-coin-top-desktop-gyro">FS-139 6063 Aluminum Coin-Top R188 Bearing Desktop Gyro</a> ($4.56–$5.62)—is spun via compressed air nozzle to verify zero vertical wobble (<0.05mm axial runout) and quiet operation (<35 dB).</li>
  <li><strong>Test #8 — Multi-Axis Gimbal Ring Clearance Check:</strong> On 22-part concentric gyros like the <a href="/products/6-axis-cnc-stainless-gyro-spinner">FS-043 22-Part 6-Axis CNC Stainless Steel Mechanical Gyro Spinner</a> ($37.16–$45.85) and <a href="/products/rainbow-pvd-stainless-gimbal-gyro-model-9">FS-120 Rainbow PVD Stainless Steel Multi-Axis Gimbal Gyro Model-9</a> ($26.92–$33.22), each nested ring is rotated 360 degrees to confirm zero binding across all 6 axes.</li>
</ul>
