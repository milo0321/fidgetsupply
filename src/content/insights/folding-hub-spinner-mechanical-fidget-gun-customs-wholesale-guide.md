<h2 id="spring-fatigue-and-mechanical-qc-standards">4. Spring Fatigue Life, Detent Tolerance &amp; Pre-Shipment Mechanical Inspection</h2>

  <table class="geo-table">
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Mechanical QC Checkpoint</th>
        <th>Engineering Standard</th>
        <th>Failure Mode Prevented</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td><strong>Spring Wire Alloy</strong></td>
        <td>SUS304 spring steel or C5191 phosphor bronze</td>
        <td>Prevents permanent spring compression ("mushy click") after 5,000+ actuations</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td><strong>Detent Ball Hardness</strong></td>
        <td>HRC 60+ hardened bearing steel or Si3N4 ceramic</td>
        <td>Eliminates flat-spotting and metallic galling inside aluminum tracks</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td><strong>Fastener Threadlocking</strong></td>
