<h2 id="comparing-three-industrial-branding-methods">2. Why Our Factory Does NOT Recommend Silk-Screen / UV Printing on Metal (And What We Recommend Instead)</h2>

Many promotional buyers initially ask for standard silk-screen or flatbed UV surface printing on metal fidget sliders. **However, our factory engineering team explicitly advises against surface silk-screen or UV ink printing on metal EDC fidgets.**

Here is the metallurgical reason:

Polished, electroplated, anodized, and PVD-coated metal surfaces (`Zamak #3 Zinc Alloy`, `304/316 Stainless Steel`, `6063 Aluminum`, `Brass`, and `Zirconium`) have **ultra-low surface roughness (`Ra < 0.2 μm`) and high chemical inertness**. Because metal is non-porous and chemically inert, surface-applied silk-screen or UV inks suffer from **insufficient interfacial adhesion**—the ink cannot bite into the metal substrate. Under the continuous thumb friction, skin oils, and pocket abrasion that an EDC fidget endures thousands of times per week, surface-printed ink inevitably delaminates, chips, and peels off.

Therefore, our factory strictly recommends two permanent laser-based processes:
1. **Standard Monochrome Logo → 50W MOPA Fiber Laser Engraving** (**+$0.15–$0.30 USD/pc**, **$50 USD** sample fee).
2. **Colored Brand Logo → Deep Laser Engraving + Enamel Color Fill** (**+$0.35–$0.60 USD/pc**, **$80–$150 USD** sample fee).

<h2 id="uv-color-printing-adhesion-protocol">4. How Deep Laser Engraving + Color Fill Solves the Metal Color Adhesion Problem (+$0.35–$0.60/pc)</h2>

When your brand guidelines require specific Pantone colors rather than a monochrome laser mark, surface silk-screen or UV ink is not viable because smooth metal has high chemical inertness and zero pore absorption—causing surface ink to peel off.

Our factory solves this permanently through **Deep Laser Engraving + Enamel Color Fill**:

<ol>
  <li><strong>Stage 1 — Multi-Pass Deep Laser Cavity Carving (`0.15mm – 0.25mm` Depth):</strong>
    <ul>
      <li>Instead of lightly marking the surface, our 50W fiber laser executes multiple high-energy ablation passes to carve a physical recessed trench (**0.15mm to 0.25mm deep**) directly into the metal shell.</li>
      <li>The bottom of this laser-carved trench has a micro-textured roughness (`Ra 2.5–4.0 μm`) that creates massive mechanical anchor tooth inside the metal.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Stage 2 — Precision Enamel Color Inlay &amp; Thermal Curing:</strong>
    <ul>
      <li>Technicians fill the recessed laser cavity with industrial two-part colored enamel pigment matched to your Pantone (PMS) code and thermally cure the piece.</li>
      <li>Because the cured color sits **below the surrounding metal surface plane**, the user's thumb slides across the raised metal rim rather than rubbing directly against the edge of the paint. The color is mechanically locked inside the metal cavity and never peels off.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Transparent Factory Pricing &amp; Sampling:</strong>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Bulk Unit Cost:</strong> **+$0.35 to $0.60 USD per piece** (depending on logo area and color count) at a **100 to 200 pcs MOQ**.</li>
      <li><strong>Paid Pre-Production Sample Fee:</strong> **$80 to $150 USD** (10–15 days lead time), compared to **$50 USD** for a standard monochrome laser sample.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>
