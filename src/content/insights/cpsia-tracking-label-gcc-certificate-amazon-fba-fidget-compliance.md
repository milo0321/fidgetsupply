<h2 id="gcc-vs-cpc-and-14-plus-age-grading-matrix">2. GCC vs. CPC vs. CPSIA Tracking Labels: Which Document Applies to Your Metal Fidget SKU?</h2>

<p>Under the US Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act (CPSIA), products intended primarily for children <strong>12 years of age or younger</strong> require a Children's Product Certificate (CPC), third-party CPSC-accepted lab testing (ASTM F963), and permanent CPSIA tracking labels. By contrast, heavy precision metal EDC desk instruments graded and marketed strictly for <strong>Ages 14+ / Adult Everyday Carry</strong> fall under General Use rules—requiring a <strong>General Certificate of Conformity (GCC)</strong> for <strong>16 CFR Part 1262</strong> (if magnetic) rather than a children's CPC:</p>

<h2 id="mandatory-seven-elements-of-a-valid-gcc-and-tracking-label">3. The 7 Mandatory Fields of an Audit-Ready GCC &amp; Outer Box Tracking Label</h2>

<p>When Amazon Seller Central requests a <strong>General Certificate of Conformity (GCC)</strong> for magnetic sliders such as <a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125 Smooth Soap-Box Slider</a>, <a href="/products/minimalist-smooth-6063-aluminum-slider">FS-047 6063 Aluminum Slider</a>, <a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013 304SS Haptic Coin</a>, <a href="/products/wheel-rim-12-magnet-haptic-coin">FS-030 Wheel Rim Coin</a>, <a href="/products/delta-triangle-magnetic-slider">FS-006 Delta Triangle Slider</a>, or <a href="/products/compact-20-magnet-acoustic-tuning-fork-slider">FS-150 Acoustic Tuning Fork Slider</a>, automated reviewers verify seven exact fields. Missing even one field results in automatic rejection:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Exact Product Identification:</strong> Must match your Amazon ASIN title, SKU/model code (e.g., FS-125), material, and finish word-for-word.</li>
  <li><strong>Exact Safety Rule Citation:</strong> Explicitly cite <code>16 CFR Part 1262 — Safety Standard for Magnets</code> (and <code>RoHS 3 Directive 2015/863</code> / <code>CA Prop 65</code> for surface finishes).</li>
  <li><strong>US Importer / Domestic Brand Contact:</strong> Your US LLC name, physical street address (no P.O. boxes), email, and phone number.</li>
  <li><strong>Records Custodian Contact:</strong> Name, email, and phone number of the person maintaining test reports at your company.</li>
  <li><strong>Date &amp; Place of Manufacture:</strong> Month/Year (e.g., <code>11/2026</code>) and city/province (<code>Fuzhou, Fujian, China</code>) matching the batch code printed on your outer box.</li>
  <li><strong>Date &amp; Place of Testing:</strong> Month/Year and lab location where 16 CFR Part 1262 drop, torque, tension, and flux-index tests were executed.</li>
  <li><strong>Third-Party Lab Identification (if applicable):</strong> Lab name, address, and report reference number.</li>
</ol>
