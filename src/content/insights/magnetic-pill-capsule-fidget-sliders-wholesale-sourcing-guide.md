<p>Cylindrical EDC items place different manufacturing demands on metalworkers compared to flat two-plate sliders. When CNC-turned on automated Swiss lathes, outer knurling, circumferential grooves, and M16 internal threads must remain concentric within 0.02 mm so caps close flush without squeaking or catching.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Type-II Color Anodizing (6063-T6 Aluminum):</strong> Creates a micro-porous oxide skin dyed in tactical black, titanium grey, slate blue, or olive drab before hot-water nickel sealing. Because the dye lives inside the grown oxide layer—not painted on top—it survives keys and coins in pockets.</li>
  <li><strong>360° Synchronized Rotary Fiber Laser Engraving:</strong> On curved barrels like <a href="/products/6063-aluminum-magnetic-capsule-pill">FS-012</a> and <a href="/products/anodized-aluminum-12-magnet-gourd-edc">FS-096</a>, mounting pieces on a motorized CNC rotary chuck keeps your company logo or wordmark crisp (+$0.15–$0.30/pc at MOQ 100) without optical focal distortion at the edges.</li>
