<h2 id="four-engineering-methods-that-eliminate-metallic-impact-noise">3. The 4 Engineering Mechanisms That Cut Metal Fidget Noise Below `45 dBA`</h2>

How do we preserve strong, satisfying tactile feedback for ADHD focus and anxiety relief while eliminating high-frequency metallic clatter? Our engineers combine four acoustic damping techniques:

1. **Non-Contact Magnetic Shear Indexing (`Zero Metal-on-Metal Impact`)**: In our wearable <a href="/collections/magnetic-fidget-rings">Magnetic Spinner Rings</a> (<a href="/products/machinist-tri-metal-magnetic-spinner-ring">FS-014</a>, <a href="/products/aluminum-brass-16-magnet-spinner-ring">FS-135</a>, <a href="/products/m-profile-aluminum-16-magnet-ring">FS-140</a>), 16 miniature neodymium magnets are sealed inside opposing inner and outer bands separated by a precision `0.25mm` radial air gap. As you spin the outer ring with your thumb, your finger feels **16 distinct magnetic pulses per revolution**, yet because the magnets never physically collide, acoustic output stays under **36 dBA**!
2. **Virgin PTFE (Teflon) Elastomeric Shear Buffers**: On sliders like <a href="/products/bionic-bunny-ptfe-spring-mechanical-slider">FS-127 (Bionic Bunny PTFE Spring Mechanical Slider)</a>, <a href="/products/retro-camera-ptfe-magnetic-slider">FS-016</a>, and PTFE-equipped <a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125</a>, a `0.30mm` virgin polytetrafluoroethylene membrane sits between the sliding plates—preventing metal-on-metal acoustic transient spikes and shifting the click frequency down into a warm, muted thud (see our <a href="/insights/ptfe-teflon-tape-ceramic-beads-r188-bearing-fidget-maintenance-guide">PTFE Friction Pad &amp; Maintenance Guide</a>).
3. **Silicone Elastomer Bumpers &amp; Amorphous PEI Ultem Shells**: <a href="/products/18-magnet-silicone-bumper-fidget-spinner">FS-145 (18-Magnet Silicone Bumper Spinner)</a> surrounds the metal rotor with a shock-absorbing food-grade silicone ring, while <a href="/products/amber-pei-ultem-cnc-gourd-edc">FS-099 (Amber PEI Ultem CNC Gourd)</a> replaces resonant metal walls with high-internal-damping polyetherimide polymer (see <a href="/insights/zinc-alloy-die-casting-vs-cnc-stainless-steel-edc-cost">Metallurgy &amp; PEI Ultem Guide</a>).
4. **10-Ball 440C Stainless Steel R188 Desktop Gyros**: Pure rotational instruments in our <a href="/collections/metal-fidget-spinners-gyros">Metal Fidget Spinners &amp; Gyros collection</a> (<a href="/products/6063-aluminum-coin-top-desktop-gyro">FS-139</a>, <a href="/products/anodized-aluminum-spinning-desk-egg">FS-064</a>, <a href="/products/3d-multi-axis-stainless-gimbal-gyro">FS-066</a>) generate smooth gyroscopic precession in the palm at **34–38 dBA**—completely invisible to Zoom noise-cancellation algorithms (see <a href="/insights/r188-bearing-metal-fidget-spinners-desktop-gyro-engineering-guide">R188 Bearing &amp; Gyro Engineering Guide</a>).
