<h2 id="how-we-pass-iata-dgr-pi953-magnetic-air-freight-inspection">3. Engineering IATA Packing Instruction 953 (PI-953) Compliance for N52/N54 Magnet Shipments</h2>

Under **International Air Transport Association (IATA) Dangerous Goods Regulations Packing Instruction 953**, any air cargo package containing permanent magnets (<a href="/collections/magnetic-fidget-sliders">Magnetic Sliders</a>, <a href="/collections/haptic-fidget-coins">Haptic Coins</a>, <a href="/collections/magnetic-fidget-rings">Spinner Rings</a>) is classified as **UN 2807 (Magnetized Material)** if its stray magnetic field exceeds `0.00525 Gauss` (`0.525 milliGauss`, equivalent to a `2°` compass deflection) at a distance of `2.1 meters (7 feet)` from any outer surface of the master carton.

To qualify as **Unrestricted General Air Cargo (Exempt under PI-953 Section II)** so your shipment flies on any passenger or cargo aircraft without dangerous-goods surcharges, our Fuzhou export packing line executes a **3-Stage Magnetic Flux Containment Protocol**:

1. **Antiparallel Dipole Checkerboard Stacking**: Individual boxes inside the master carton are oriented in alternating `North-Up / South-Up` columns so adjacent dipole fields cancel each other out within a `5cm` radius.
2. **0.35mm Cold-Rolled Silicon-Iron Shielding Cage**: For high-magnet-count models—such as <a href="/products/triple-pea-pod-39-magnet-4-bearing-edc">FS-136 (39 Magnets)</a>, <a href="/products/drop-tower-6-bearing-24-magnet-heavy-edc">FS-142 (24 Magnets + 6 Bearings)</a>, <a href="/products/abalone-inlay-56-magnet-stainless-slider">FS-091 (56 Magnets)</a>, and <a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125 (20 × N52 Magnets)</a>—we line all 6 inner faces of the 5-ply export carton with `0.35mm` high-permeability ferromagnetic silicon-iron plates taped into a closed Faraday/flux loop.
3. **Pre-Dispatch Digital Gaussmeter Certification (`DGM Report`)**: Before sealing the carton, our QC technician scans all 6 faces at `2.1m` using a calibrated 3-axis fluxgate milligaussmeter, verifying that peak stray field remains strictly below `0.0035 Gauss` (see our <a href="/insights/n35-vs-n52-neodymium-magnet-haptic-feel-guide">N35 vs. N52 Magnet Guide</a> and <a href="/insights/custom-eva-zipper-cases-tin-boxes-metal-fidget-packaging-guide">Custom EVA &amp; Tin Box Packaging Guide</a>).
