<h2 id="permanent-laser-over-pad-ink">3. Permanent 50W Fiber Laser vs. Deep Enamel Color Fill: Eliminating Logo Peeling</h2>

Polished and electroplated metal substrates are chemically inert and ultra-smooth, so surface-sitting inks lack mechanical anchor and rub away within days of thumb sliding.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Monochrome 50W MOPA Fiber Laser (+$0.15–$0.30/pc, MOQ 100):</strong> Vaporizes microns of anodized coating or thermally anneals high-contrast dark marks directly into raw 304 stainless steel—surviving years of pocket carry with $0 setup fees in bulk runs.</li>
