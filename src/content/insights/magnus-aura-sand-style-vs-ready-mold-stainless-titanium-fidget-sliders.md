<h2 id="surface-machining-and-ptfe-mechanics">3. Why Smooth Metal Plate Sliders Require Virgin PTFE Liners &amp; Chamfered Pockets</h2>

Every FidgetSupply precision slider integrates replaceable <strong>0.30 mm virgin PTFE Teflon glide pads</strong> (as seen on <a href="/products/abalone-relief-cnc-stainless-rhythm-slider">FS-094 / FS-095</a> and <a href="/products/compact-20-magnet-acoustic-tuning-fork-slider">FS-150</a>) or concealed ceramic detent rails (<a href="/products/tiger-totem-ceramic-detent-slider">FS-042</a>) that isolate wear to low-cost replaceable liners rather than the machined steel body.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>2-Layer Unlimited Trick Sliders (<a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125</a>, <a href="/products/blade-scar-26-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-073</a>, <a href="/products/infinity-cube-26-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-050</a>):</strong> Smooth perimeter chamfers allow 180° rotational fan tricks and free-floating diagonal clicks favored by Aura Sand and MagLab trick enthusiasts.</li>
