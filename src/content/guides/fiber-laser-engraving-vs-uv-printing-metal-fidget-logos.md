<h2 id="mopa-fiber-laser-physics-and-substrates">3. Why 50W MOPA Fiber Laser Engraving Is the Gold Standard for Monochrome Logos</h2>

Unlike standard Q-switched lasers that operate at fixed pulse durations, our **50W MOPA (Master Oscillator Power Amplifier) fiber lasers** adjust pulse width between **2 nanoseconds and 500 nanoseconds** at frequencies up to **2,000 kHz**.

Why does pulse-width control matter for your brand logo?

<ul>
  <li><strong>Razor-Sharp Bright White Marks on Anodized Aluminum (<a href="/products/minimalist-smooth-6063-aluminum-slider">FS-047</a>, <a href="/products/anodized-aluminum-spinning-desk-egg">FS-064</a>, <a href="/products/anodized-aluminum-12-magnet-gourd-edc">FS-096</a>):</strong> Short 4ns pulses strip the colored Type-II anodized dye layer cleanly down to the bright white aluminum oxide barrier without melting the metal grain or leaving rough thermal edges.</li>
  <li><strong>Zero-Burr Black Annealing on 304/316 Stainless Steel (<a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013</a>, <a href="/products/304ss-akq-triple-layer-24-magnet-slider">FS-075</a>, <a href="/products/harmonic-rhythm-cnc-stainless-slider">FS-086</a>):</strong> High-frequency nanosecond pulses create a smooth, high-contrast dark chromium-oxide mark on stonewashed or brushed stainless steel that stays completely smooth under the user's thumb.</li>
  <li><strong>360-Degree Rotary Engraving on Gourds &amp; Spinner Rings (<a href="/products/machinist-tri-metal-magnetic-spinner-ring">FS-014</a>, <a href="/products/aluminum-brass-16-magnet-spinner-ring">FS-135</a>):</strong> Cylindrical gourds and the inner/outer bezels of mechanical rings lock into a stepper-driven rotary chuck for distortion-free rotational logos and edition numbers.</li>
  <li><strong>Automated CSV Edition Serialization (`001/300`):</strong> Collector EDC drops thrive on scarcity. Our EZCAD Galvo laser system increments sequential numbers (`001/300` through `300/300`) or individual VIP recipient names directly from a spreadsheet for just **+$0.20 per piece**.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="uv-color-printing-adhesion-protocol">4. Two Ways to Produce Colored Logos: Silk-Screen Printing vs. Deep Laser Engraving + Color Fill</h2>

When your brand guidelines call for specific Pantone colors rather than a monochrome laser mark, we offer two distinct factory processes depending on your budget, quantity, and desired tactile finish:

<ol>
  <li><strong>Method A — Multi-Color Silk-Screen Printing (Standard Colored Logo Process):</strong>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Screen Setup Fee ($35 per Color):</strong> Each spot color requires its own precision mesh stencil screen at **$35 USD per color**. Multi-color logos stack linearly: 1 color = **$35**, 2 colors = **2 × $35 = $70**, 3 colors = **3 × $35 = $105**.</li>
      <li><strong>Unit Printing Cost (+$0.10 per Color / pc):</strong> Each color pass adds **+$0.10 USD per unit** to the base product price. A 1-color logo adds **+$0.10/pc**, a 2-color logo adds **+$0.20/pc**, and a 5-color logo adds **5 × $0.10 = +$0.50/pc**.</li>
      <li><strong>Best For:</strong> Flat-surface sliders like <a href="/products/smooth-soap-box-20-magnet-unlimited-slider">FS-125</a>, <a href="/products/poker-king-magnetic-slider">FS-001</a>, and <a href="/products/bottle-opener-12-magnet-edc-slider">FS-026</a> where crisp, cost-effective Pantone color reproduction is needed starting at 100 pcs.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>Method B — Deep Laser Engraving + Enamel Color Fill (MOQ 200–300 pcs):</strong>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Two-Stage Recessed Process:</strong> First, our high-power fiber laser performs multiple deep-ablation passes to carve a recessed cavity (**0.12mm to 0.25mm deep**) into the metal shell. Second, technicians fill the recessed engraving with two-part colored enamel ink and oven-cure the piece so the pigment sits securely below the metal surface plane.</li>
      <li><strong>MOQ &amp; Unit Pricing:</strong> Available at a **200 to 300 pieces MOQ** per SKU, with a unit branding cost of **+$0.35 to $0.60 USD per piece** (depending on logo area and color count).</li>
      <li><strong>Best For:</strong> Collector coins (<a href="/products/304ss-12-magnet-haptic-fidget-coin">FS-013</a>), CNC stainless sliders (<a href="/products/304ss-akq-triple-layer-24-magnet-slider">FS-075</a>, <a href="/products/harmonic-rhythm-cnc-stainless-slider">FS-086</a>), and anodized aluminum models (<a href="/products/minimalist-smooth-6063-aluminum-slider">FS-047</a>) where you want permanent recessed color that never rubs off under heavy thumb friction.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>

<h2 id="artwork-preparation-checklist">6. Vector Artwork Preparation &amp; Dual-Branding with Custom EVA Cases</h2>

To ensure your 12-hour digital proof and 10–15 day custom logo sample ($80–$150 USD sample fee) proceed without revision delays, follow these four pre-press guidelines:

<ul>
  <li><strong>Vector File Formats:</strong> Submit artwork in `.AI`, `.EPS`, vector `.PDF`, or `.SVG` format with all text converted to curves/outlines.</li>
  <li><strong>Minimum Line Weight &amp; Font Height:</strong> For 50W Fiber Laser Engraving, maintain a minimum stroke thickness of **0.15 mm** and minimum letter height of **1.2 mm**. For CMYK+W UV printing, raster graphics should be at least **300 dpi at 1:1 print size**.</li>
